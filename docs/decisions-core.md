# Core decisions

Decisions made where `CONTEXT.md` is silent or ambiguous, chosen to fit its principles and guardrails.

| ID    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D-001 | TypeScript 6.0, not 7.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | typescript-eslint (type-checked lint rules) supports TypeScript < 6.1.                                                                                                                                                                |
| D-002 | Money is integer cents (`bigint`) internally and two-decimal strings on the wire; percentages are integer basis points internally. Discounts round half up to the cent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | No floats in money (CONTEXT.md). Half-up is the conventional rounding for invoice amounts; every CONTEXT.md example is exact under it.                                                                                                |
| D-003 | Annual rates are compared to hurdles exactly (rational arithmetic) and only rounded for display (one decimal, half up). The counter "at the target rate" rounds to the nearest 0.1%, half up.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A displayed "15.0%" must never accept an offer that is really 14.96%.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D-004 | The deal state machine extends the CONTEXT.md table: `REMOVED` (owner drops a row before approval); `APPROVED → WITHDRAWN/FAILED`; `COUNTERED/NEEDS_OWNER → EXPIRED/WITHDRAWN`; `INVOICE_UPDATED → FAILED`; any open state → `PAID`/`PARTIALLY_PAID` when PayPal confirms a payment; `PARTIALLY_PAID → PAID` when the rest lands. Only the `PAYPAL` actor may move a deal to a paid state; only `OWNER` approves or removes a row; only `SYSTEM` records invoice updates.                                       | Offers expire and the stop rule withdraws open offers, so those exits must exist. A client can pay the original invoice without taking the offer; PayPal is the source of truth for money, so the deal must reflect it.               |
| D-005 | Client cash-buffer check passes when cash after paying is **at least** the buffer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | CONTEXT.md says both "above the buffer" and "never pay early below it"; the second is the enforcement rule.                                                                                                                           |
| D-006 | Auto-pay limit is compared to the **payment** (amount early minus discount); the early-pay cap to the **amount early**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Matches the scenario: Juniper's $4,960 payment exceeds its $3,000 limit; Harbor's $7,500 invoice exceeds its $5,000 cap.                                                                                                              |
| D-007 | Public offer JSON is served at `/api/offer/:token`; the SPA owns `/offer/:token`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | One Render service serves both the API and the built frontend; the same path cannot return both HTML and JSON.                                                                                                                        |
| D-008 | Added endpoints: health, businesses, session, connection, `goals/current`, `GET goals/:id`, `GET plans/:id`, `GET deals`, supplier policy, `GET client/policy`, `GET client/vault`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Needed for demo sessions per business, the Connect and Settings screens, and loading the Workbench; not listed in CONTEXT.md but implied by its screens.                                                                              |
| D-009 | `ClientPolicy` gains `targetRatePct`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | CONTEXT.md's client policy and strategy use a target rate, but the table omits the column.                                                                                                                                            |
| D-010 | Actor `CLIENT_HUMAN` added.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Human client replies and client-side payment approvals must be distinguishable from the client agent in the audit trail.                                                                                                              |
| D-011 | Reminders are deals with action `REMINDER` that never count toward committed cash (`countsTowardGoal: false`); if the invoice is then paid, PayPal's confirmation moves the deal to `PAID` and the cash counts as paid.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | "Every action has a visible state" and "reminders are never counted as committed cash".                                                                                                                                               |
| D-012 | `packages/shared` is consumed from source via the `@terms/source` export condition in development and from `dist/` in production.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | No watch build needed in development; production runs plain JavaScript.                                                                                                                                                               |
| D-013 | `ClientPolicy.cashOnHandCents` holds the client's own declared cash figure, which its agent uses for the cash-buffer check.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The demo app only holds the supplier's PayPal credentials, so it cannot read a client's PayPal balance. The figure is the client's own treasury data, never shown to the supplier.                                                    |
| D-014 | Planner search runs on a $1 grid (CONTEXT.md suggests $10), rounding each offer's committed cash down. The plan is exactly optimal on that grid and never claims coverage it lacks; a cover within a few dollars of the boundary may cost one more step. The grid coarsens by 10× only above 200,000 states (targets over $200,000).                                                                                                                                                                            | Ten times finer than the spec while staying fast; rounding down keeps "only committed cash counts" true in exact cents.                                                                                                               |
| D-015 | Offers ask for payment by the plan date (today), so days early = due date − today. Backups are full-amount offers at the opening discount on unused eligible invoices, ordered by cost then dollar-days. When the target is unreachable, the plan asks every eligible client for its full invoice and is flagged `shortfall`.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Reproduces the scenario's 16.4% and 12.3% rates (18 and 24 days). Backups stay simple and cheap; the deal engine re-plans exactly when an offer is declined.                                                                          |
| D-016 | The supplier agent **accepts** a client counter that is within d_max and keeps the plan under its ceiling, instead of sending a counter with identical terms (CONTEXT.md: "counter at min(d_max, counter)"). A counter above d_max is answered with a counter at d_max. Either way, a move that would cross the cost ceiling becomes NEEDS_OWNER; if the owner approves, the prepared accept or counter goes out. Whoever accepts, a payment above the client's auto-pay limit needs the client's person (I-4). | min(d_max, c) = c when c ≤ d_max, so the counter would restate the client's terms; accepting is the same agreement in one round fewer. The demo flow is unchanged: 1.5% → counter at 1.2%, $1 over → owner → Harbor accepts.          |
| D-017 | Goal parsing has a rule-based mode (`apps/api/src/goals/goal-parser.ts`) used whenever no LLM is configured: amounts, deadlines, the maximum discount, a buffer and do-not-contact names. Fields it cannot find come from the owner's policy and are listed in `defaultedFields`; the owner confirms or edits every field before planning.                                                                                                                                                                      | Mirrors the negotiation strategies' deterministic mode (CONTEXT.md requires one for tests and the seeded scenario); the demo flow never depends on an external model. The LLM parser, when added, is held to the same schema.         |
| D-018 | Demo sessions run on the wall clock; only business logic uses the configurable clock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | A session cookie's expiry is read by the browser on real time; business dates ("today", offer expiry) must be controllable in tests.                                                                                                  |
| D-019 | Live `deal.updated` events are published by callers after their transaction commits (`publishDeals`), not from inside transitions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A listener inside a transaction would show screens a state that can still roll back.                                                                                                                                                  |
| D-020 | The runway's "before" line is the PayPal balance plus the owner's confirmed recurring flows only; receivables count only once committed ("after" lines), and reminders only as best effort. Payments PayPal confirmed after its balance timestamp are added to the balance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Only committed cash counts (principle 4); Transaction Search lags about two hours (spike S4).                                                                                                                                         |
| D-021 | The seed records Maya's payroll as an owner-confirmed recurring flow ($11,400 on Friday) rather than creating past payroll payouts in the sandbox.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Real payouts of $11,400 would drain Maya's $5,200 sandbox balance that the scenario depends on; the flow is the owner's own declaration, labelled CONFIRMED_RECURRING.                                                                |
| D-022 | Human replies on the offer page are buttons (pay now, pay part, not now). Free-text replies answer NOT_CONFIGURED until the LLM reply parser exists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The reply parser is an LLM role; without it, guessing at free text could change terms the person did not intend.                                                                                                                      |
| D-023 | On a shared deal, a move whose real reason comes from one side's policy stores two reasons: a neutral public one (history, `closedReason`, the other side's audit) and the real one for that side only (`DealTransition.privateReason`/`privateTo`). Message notes carry terms, never an agent's reasons. Policy checks show only to the side that ran them, and approvals only to the business they are addressed to.                                                                                          | Each business's policy (ceiling, target, auto-pay limit, never-pay-early list) is its own; seeing it would let the other side negotiate against it. CR-4.                                                                             |
| D-024 | A model call never happens inside a database transaction. The engine decides (and writes the note) first, then opens the transaction, checks the deal's version is unchanged and applies the move; a note for an owner approval is stored on the approval (`draftNote`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Prisma's interactive transactions time out at 5 s and a Serializable transaction held open for a provider call would block other writers. Re-checking the version keeps the move consistent with the deal it was chosen for.          |
| D-025 | Every AI role has a deterministic fallback and fails soft: a missing key, provider error, timeout or refused answer yields the rule-based parser, the standard strategy, the plain note, the planner's reason or PayPal's wording, never a blocked owner. Routes that cannot fall back (a written offer reply, the Studio) answer 502 `AI_ERROR` and change nothing.                                                                                                                                            | The product must work, and be demoable, without a key or when a provider is down (CONTEXT.md: deterministic mode).                                                                                                                    |
| D-026 | Figures in model-written text are checked by kind (dollars, percentages, other numbers), not as a single set of numbers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A single set let the 9 in a date vouch for an invented 9%; found by the note writer's own test.                                                                                                                                       |
| D-027 | The flow labeller only labels history for display. It never confirms a recurring flow, so the runway is unchanged by it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | The runway counts only flows the owner confirmed (D-020); a model's guess must not move a forecast.                                                                                                                                   |
| D-028 | Recorded AI answers are keyed by role, tier, system text and prompt, and replayed at the answer level (not the SDK's wire level). The Studio records a whole turn, including the data its tools returned.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Replay then survives SDK upgrades, and a demo is deterministic. Prompts are part of the key, so changing a prompt means re-recording.                                                                                                 |
| D-029 | A free-text offer reply can only trigger the offer page's own actions, and only when the model reports it clear; it cannot change the discount or any other term. A promise to pay is noted, not counted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The reply comes from a counterparty; the model's reading must never widen what that person can do (principle 4, only committed cash counts).                                                                                          |
| D-030 | The Cash Studio's what-if runway is computed on the server and returned as `scenario` on the turn response, from the planner's simulation (offers as open deals on their pay-by dates). Only that route gets a 1 MB body limit; a tool result over 32 KB is replaced by a preview rather than rejected.                                                                                                                                                                                                         | The browser must not work out payment outcomes (B's CR-5), and a widget toolset's JSON Schema alone is larger than the 100 KB default. A failing chart would break the conversation, so oversize results degrade instead of erroring. |
