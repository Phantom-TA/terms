# Core decisions

Decisions made where `CONTEXT.md` is silent or ambiguous, chosen to fit its principles and guardrails.

| ID    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D-001 | TypeScript 6.0, not 7.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | typescript-eslint (type-checked lint rules) supports TypeScript < 6.1.                                                                                                                                                       |
| D-002 | Money is integer cents (`bigint`) internally and two-decimal strings on the wire; percentages are integer basis points internally. Discounts round half up to the cent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | No floats in money (CONTEXT.md). Half-up is the conventional rounding for invoice amounts; every CONTEXT.md example is exact under it.                                                                                       |
| D-003 | Annual rates are compared to hurdles exactly (rational arithmetic) and only rounded for display (one decimal, half up). The counter "at the target rate" rounds to the nearest 0.1%, half up.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A displayed "15.0%" must never accept an offer that is really 14.96%.                                                                                                                                                        |
| D-004 | The deal state machine extends the CONTEXT.md table: `REMOVED` (owner drops a row before approval); `APPROVED → WITHDRAWN/FAILED`; `COUNTERED/NEEDS_OWNER → EXPIRED/WITHDRAWN`; `INVOICE_UPDATED → FAILED`; any open state → `PAID`/`PARTIALLY_PAID` when PayPal confirms a payment; `PARTIALLY_PAID → PAID` when the rest lands. Only the `PAYPAL` actor may move a deal to a paid state; only `OWNER` approves or removes a row; only `SYSTEM` records invoice updates.                                       | Offers expire and the stop rule withdraws open offers, so those exits must exist. A client can pay the original invoice without taking the offer; PayPal is the source of truth for money, so the deal must reflect it.      |
| D-005 | Client cash-buffer check passes when cash after paying is **at least** the buffer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | CONTEXT.md says both "above the buffer" and "never pay early below it"; the second is the enforcement rule.                                                                                                                  |
| D-006 | Auto-pay limit is compared to the **payment** (amount early minus discount); the early-pay cap to the **amount early**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Matches the scenario: Juniper's $4,960 payment exceeds its $3,000 limit; Harbor's $7,500 invoice exceeds its $5,000 cap.                                                                                                     |
| D-007 | Public offer JSON is served at `/api/offer/:token`; the SPA owns `/offer/:token`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | One Render service serves both the API and the built frontend; the same path cannot return both HTML and JSON.                                                                                                               |
| D-008 | Added endpoints: health, businesses, session, connection, `goals/current`, `GET goals/:id`, `GET plans/:id`, `GET deals`, supplier policy, `GET client/policy`, `GET client/vault`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Needed for demo sessions per business, the Connect and Settings screens, and loading the Workbench; not listed in CONTEXT.md but implied by its screens.                                                                     |
| D-009 | `ClientPolicy` gains `targetRatePct`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | CONTEXT.md's client policy and strategy use a target rate, but the table omits the column.                                                                                                                                   |
| D-010 | Actor `CLIENT_HUMAN` added.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Human client replies and client-side payment approvals must be distinguishable from the client agent in the audit trail.                                                                                                     |
| D-011 | Reminders are deals with action `REMINDER` that never count toward committed cash (`countsTowardGoal: false`); if the invoice is then paid, PayPal's confirmation moves the deal to `PAID` and the cash counts as paid.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | "Every action has a visible state" and "reminders are never counted as committed cash".                                                                                                                                      |
| D-012 | `packages/shared` is consumed from source via the `@terms/source` export condition in development and from `dist/` in production.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | No watch build needed in development; production runs plain JavaScript.                                                                                                                                                      |
| D-013 | `ClientPolicy.cashOnHandCents` holds the client's own declared cash figure, which its agent uses for the cash-buffer check.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The demo app only holds the supplier's PayPal credentials, so it cannot read a client's PayPal balance. The figure is the client's own treasury data, never shown to the supplier.                                           |
| D-014 | Planner search runs on a $1 grid (CONTEXT.md suggests $10), rounding each offer's committed cash down. The plan is exactly optimal on that grid and never claims coverage it lacks; a cover within a few dollars of the boundary may cost one more step. The grid coarsens by 10× only above 200,000 states (targets over $200,000).                                                                                                                                                                            | Ten times finer than the spec while staying fast; rounding down keeps "only committed cash counts" true in exact cents.                                                                                                      |
| D-015 | Offers ask for payment by the plan date (today), so days early = due date − today. Backups are full-amount offers at the opening discount on unused eligible invoices, ordered by cost then dollar-days. When the target is unreachable, the plan asks every eligible client for its full invoice and is flagged `shortfall`.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Reproduces the scenario's 16.4% and 12.3% rates (18 and 24 days). Backups stay simple and cheap; the deal engine re-plans exactly when an offer is declined.                                                                 |
| D-016 | The supplier agent **accepts** a client counter that is within d_max and keeps the plan under its ceiling, instead of sending a counter with identical terms (CONTEXT.md: "counter at min(d_max, counter)"). A counter above d_max is answered with a counter at d_max. Either way, a move that would cross the cost ceiling becomes NEEDS_OWNER; if the owner approves, the prepared accept or counter goes out. Whoever accepts, a payment above the client's auto-pay limit needs the client's person (I-4). | min(d_max, c) = c when c ≤ d_max, so the counter would restate the client's terms; accepting is the same agreement in one round fewer. The demo flow is unchanged: 1.5% → counter at 1.2%, $1 over → owner → Harbor accepts. |
